TM7 Egg Fried Rice 💎

Serves 3–4 · TM7 · Side dish

Simple egg fried rice designed to go with sticky chilli beef or other Chinese-style dishes.

Ingredients

  • 300 g jasmine rice
  • 1,000 g water
  • 3 eggs
  • 20 g neutral cooking oil
  • 3 spring onions, sliced, white and green parts separated
  • 100 g frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ¼ tsp MSG, optional
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Cook the rice. Rinse the jasmine rice thoroughly. Put 1,000 g water in the mixing bowl. Place the rice in the simmering basket and insert it. Cook 20 min / 100°C / speed 4. Check the rice and, if needed, cook for another 2–3 min / 100°C / speed 4. Remove the basket and spread the rice on a tray to cool. Empty and dry the mixing bowl.
  2. Cook the egg. Add 10 g oil, the eggs and a pinch of salt. Cook 4 min / 100°C / reverse / speed 1. Then mix 3 sec / reverse / speed 3. Transfer to a bowl.
  3. Cook the spring onion and peas. Add the remaining 10 g oil and the white/light-green parts of the spring onions. Cook 3 min / 120°C / reverse / speed 1. Add the frozen peas and cook 2 min / 120°C / reverse / spoon speed.
  4. Finish the rice. Add the cooled rice, cooked egg, soy sauce, sesame oil, white pepper and optional MSG. Mix 2 min / 100°C / reverse / spoon speed. Halfway through, stop and gently bring the rice from the bottom upwards with the spatula if needed. Finish with the green spring onion tops.

Best result: cool the cooked rice before the final mixing stage. Cold rice holds its texture better and is less likely to become soft.
